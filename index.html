Frequently Asked Questions

Aapke sawal, hamare jawab

Purani gaadi (Used Car) par kitna loan mil sakta hai? ▼
Hum used car finance ke liye enquiry aur documentation guidance dete hain. Loan amount gaadi ki valuation, documents, credit profile aur lender ke assessment par depend karta hai; approval guaranteed nahi hai.
Loan approval mein kitna samay lagta hai? ▼
Processing times vary by lender and application. Complete documents can help the lender review your application, but approval is not guaranteed.
Kya aap commercial vehicles (Jaise Truck/Bus) ko finance karte hain? ▼
Hum chhote tempo se lekar trucks aur buses tak, commercial vehicle finance options ke liye enquiry aur documentation guidance dete hain. Final decision lender ke assessment par depend karta hai.
Motor insurance mein kya check karna chahiye? ▼
Policy ka coverage, exclusions, deductible, insured declared value, add-ons aur renewal date dhyan se check karein. Exact benefits insurer aur policy terms par depend karte hain.
