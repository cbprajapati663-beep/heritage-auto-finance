Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Heritage Auto Finance (“we”, “our” or “us”) provides vehicle-finance enquiry assistance, documentation guidance and motor-insurance assistance. This page explains, in plain language, how information shared through this website may be handled.

1. Information you choose to share

If you submit an enquiry, you may choose to provide details such as your name, phone number, preferred service and a message about your requirements. Please provide only information that is necessary for us to respond.

2. How information may be used

  • To respond to your enquiry or callback request.
  • To understand which vehicle-finance or insurance assistance you are asking about.
  • To communicate with you about the enquiry and its next steps.
  • To help maintain and improve the website and enquiry process.

3. Forms and service providers

The enquiry form on this website is provided using HubSpot. When you use that form, information may be processed by HubSpot under its own terms and privacy policy. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp, which has its own privacy practices. Please review the applicable providers’ policies before sharing information through their services.

4. Sensitive information

Do not submit Aadhaar numbers, PAN details, bank-account credentials, passwords, OTPs, card details or copies of identity documents through a general website enquiry. If documents are needed later, confirm the recipient and a suitable secure channel first.

5. Sharing and security

We do not ask for passwords or OTPs through this website. Information may be shared with a relevant lender, insurer or service provider only when needed to progress an enquiry and where appropriate. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit an enquiry. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have shared with us, contact us on +91 76002 11085 and identify the enquiry sufficiently for us to locate it. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate business obligations.

7. Children

This website is intended for people seeking vehicle-finance or insurance assistance and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when our website or enquiry process changes. The date at the top indicates the latest stated update.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Heritage Auto Finance at +91 76002 11085.
